Facet brings back the familiar app grid, quick keyboard search, and folder organization to modern macOS. Specifically designed for users who miss the classic Launchpad experience in macOS 14+ and especially **macOS 26 (Tahoe)**.

![Facet Preview](https://facet.quartz.ink/facet/hero-screenshot.png)

## Why Facet?

Apple removed Launchpad in macOS Tahoe, replacing it with a search-centric view. Facet fills that gap by providing a polished, native, and highly customizable launcher that feels like it belongs on your Mac.

### Key Features

- **Classic Grid**: Immersive full-screen or compact floating panel layout.
- **Dynamic Backgrounds**: Mirror your desktop wallpaper or use a clean frosted-glass effect.
- **Instant Access**: Appears in under 100ms via global hotkey or hot corner.
- **Smart Search**: Filter and launch apps instantly from your keyboard.
- **Import Layout**: Reads your system Launchpad database so you don't have to rebuild folders.
- **Privacy First**: No telemetry, no ad tracking, and no subscriptions. Paid once, yours forever.

## Installation

1. [Download the notarized DMG](https://facet.quartz.ink/releases/Facet-1.0.14.dmg?download=1), or run `brew install --cask QuartzInkStudio/tap/facet`.
2. Drag Facet to your `/Applications` folder.
3. Launch and set your preferred hotkey.

*Facet is Developer ID signed and Apple-notarized for a secure installation.*
