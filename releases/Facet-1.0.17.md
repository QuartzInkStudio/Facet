# Facet 1.0.17

Facet 1.0.17 adds a polished, fully functional first-run experience while keeping Facet Free permanently available and Facet Pro optional.

## What’s new

- Adds a five-step welcome flow for choosing a starting layout, launch method, fullscreen or compact mode, and Free or Pro access.
- Connects every setup choice to the real app: Launchpad import, clean layout, global shortcut, Dock icon, Pro hot corner, display mode, Free, trial, purchase, and license activation.
- Adds complete onboarding localization in English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.
- Uses the real Facet icon, a single close control, and the Facet violet visual system.

## Editions

Facet Free keeps the fullscreen grid, keyboard search and launch, folders and ordering, Launchpad import, and the global shortcut available permanently without payment.

Facet Pro is an optional $14.99 one-time upgrade for compact mode, advanced appearance and layout controls, hot corners, app hiding and aliases, and Dock or menu bar icon customization. The optional 14-day trial requires no credit card, creates no automatic charge, and returns to Facet Free when it ends.
